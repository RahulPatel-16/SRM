Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Crafted with Care

Shree Ram Masala was born from a simple belief — that great food starts with great spices. Established in 1998 in the heart of Vadodara, Gujarat, we began as a family-driven venture with one clear purpose: to bring pure, unadulterated spices to every kitchen.

We believe quality begins long before the grinding starts. Every batch of spice is then carefully processed and powdered at our own factory in Vadodara, where hygiene and purity are non-negotiable at every step.

Whether you are a home cook seeking authentic flavour or a restaurant looking for consistent quality in bulk, we are your trusted spice partner — just as we have been for the people of Vadodara and beyond since 1998.
